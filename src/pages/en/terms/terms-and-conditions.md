Welcome to Cyril Fan's personal website. It shares a personal introduction, research interests and learning notes, with email accounts for comments and no paid services.

Keep comments friendly and relevant. Do not impersonate others, post spam, disclose other people’s private information or disrupt discussions. Posting requires registration, email verification and sign-in. Comments normally publish immediately; moderation may be enabled during abuse. The owner can remove content or discussions and restrict accounts. Nicknames are not identity verification; only the server-assigned “Author” badge identifies the site owner.

Comments allow up to 2,000 characters and five web links. Regular accounts can post once per 30 seconds and five times per 10 minutes. The site owner is exempt from those two frequency limits; recent duplicate content is still rejected. Comments support Markdown, tables, task lists, code and math, with a preview before posting. External images load only when clicked, and raw HTML is shown as text. Deleting your comment preserves other people’s replies; see Privacy for data handling. The free backend may pause or become unavailable. Keep a copy of your draft and retry later, or reach out by email.

When citing a page, preserve the author, source link and context. See the [copyright notice](/en/terms/copyright) for republication and other uses. Please do not impersonate the author, remove attribution or disrupt normal access.
