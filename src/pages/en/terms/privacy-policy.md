Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We count anonymous page views without identifying unique visitors. When comments are enabled, Supabase handles authentication and the comment database, and Resend sends account verification and password recovery emails. A “being prepared” notice means that the page has not connected to the comment service.

Page views accumulate from the launch of this feature, with one total shared by both languages. Opening, refreshing or returning to a page through browser history counts once when the page becomes visible. Development previews and in-page section navigation do not count. A separate request goes to Supabase without site sign-in tokens, cookies or a referring page address. The site stores only the aggregate total, its start time, rate-limit time buckets and a random request ID for each page view. IDs are not reused across pages. We do not store visitor IP addresses, emails, device fingerprints, visited paths or referring websites in this counter. IDs prevent retries from being counted twice; IDs older than one day are periodically removed during subsequent requests. They do not identify a visitor and are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Disabled scripts, blocked requests and abnormal-traffic limits can affect the count. Page views are not a unique-person or verified-human count.

Public comments can be read without an account. Registration sends your email and password over an encrypted connection to Supabase Auth; passwords are not stored in the comment tables. Your email is used for verification, sign-in, recovery and necessary account communication, and is not shown publicly. Only the signed-in site owner with active administrator permissions can request a comment author's current account email from the management menu, for comment management and necessary contact. Other visitors and registered users cannot read it. Emails are not copied into comment content, public lists or avatar addresses. Resend processes recipient addresses and account emails, without marketing messages from this website. The owner can also manage accounts in the service dashboard. Visitors only see nicknames, comments, reply relationships, dates and owner or moderation labels. Do not post information you wish to keep private.

Your public nickname belongs to your account profile. Saving a new nickname updates how your earlier comments and reply references are displayed; other open pages show the change after refreshing. Nicknames need not be unique. The system-assigned “Site owner” badge identifies the owner, whose display name is fixed as Cyril Fan.

You may upload a public avatar; otherwise, your nickname initials are shown. The upload interface crops and compresses the image in your browser and removes the original file's metadata before sending it to Supabase avatar storage when you confirm saving. Avatars are public images that anyone with the image address can view. Only upload an image you wish to make public. Replacing or removing an avatar also changes earlier comments; other open pages reflect the change after refreshing, while caches and backups may update later. The owner's comments use the existing website portrait. Avatars are independent of email sign-in. We do not query email providers or Gravatar, and public avatar addresses do not contain email addresses, email hashes or account IDs.

Sign-in sessions normally use browser local storage. Sign out after using a shared device. If storage is unavailable, the session lasts only until the page closes or refreshes. Unsubmitted drafts remain in page memory and may be lost when leaving or refreshing. Confirmed sign-out or account changes clear the previous account's draft; a network failure alone does not.

Comments support Markdown formatting. Scripts, raw HTML and embedded pages do not run as executable content. External images show their source first and load only after a click, without sending this page's address. The image provider still receives network information such as the IP address needed to complete the request.

Deleting your comment clears its content and leaves a deletion notice, nickname, timestamp and reply relationships; other people’s replies remain. The owner may remove abusive discussions and their replies or restrict posting. Private duplicate-prevention fingerprints and operational metadata may remain; provider logs or backups follow their maintenance cycles. Clearing a comment does not immediately erase every historical copy. For account deletion or other personal data requests, contact the owner below; identity verification may be needed.

Your light or dark theme preference is stored locally in your browser for future visits. Site search queries a static index in your browser; this website has no backend that receives your search terms.

The contribution chart uses the same calendar and original snake animations as the owner's GitHub profile. This website provides a complete snapshot. While the homepage is visible, the browser also checks GitHub's public API and raw file service for updates, at most once every five minutes per page. These requests send no site account credentials or referring page address; GitHub receives the network information needed to complete them. If GitHub is unavailable, the chart checks this website's latest complete snapshot; if that also fails, it keeps the current version and its original date. Image zoom and Markdown scripts are also served by this website. Hosting and external resource providers receive the information needed to complete network requests. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for GitHub's practices. External links lead to websites with their own practices.

Friend feeds are fetched during site builds. Your browser checks this website's snapshot every ten minutes without contacting each friend's site for updates. A visible comment section checks its public list once a minute using the same permissions as ordinary comment reading. Changes produce a refresh prompt rather than replacing the content you are reading or composing.

Providers receive network information needed to deliver and protect their services, which may include IP and device information. The comment API does not publish those details. See the [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) and [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
