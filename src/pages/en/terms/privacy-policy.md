Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, without independent visitor tracking. When comments are enabled, Supabase handles authentication and the comment database, and Resend sends account verification and password recovery emails. A “being prepared” notice means that the page has not connected to the comment service.

Public comments can be read without an account. Registration sends your email and password over an encrypted connection to Supabase Auth; passwords are not stored in the comment tables. Your email is used for verification, sign-in, recovery and necessary account communication, and is not shown publicly. Resend processes recipient addresses and account emails, without marketing messages from this website. The owner can manage accounts in the service dashboard. Visitors only see nicknames, comments, reply relationships, dates and owner or moderation labels. Do not post information you wish to keep private.

Your public nickname belongs to your account profile. Saving a new nickname updates how your earlier comments and reply references are displayed; other open pages show the change after refreshing. Nicknames need not be unique. The system-assigned “Site owner” badge identifies the owner, displayed as Cyril Fan in English and 范星宇 in Chinese.

You may upload a public avatar; otherwise, your nickname initials are shown. The upload interface crops and compresses the image in your browser and removes the original file's metadata before sending it to Supabase avatar storage when you confirm saving. Avatars are public images that anyone with the image address can view. Only upload an image you wish to make public. Replacing or removing an avatar also changes earlier comments; other open pages reflect the change after refreshing, while caches and backups may update later. The owner's comments use the existing website portrait. Avatars are independent of email sign-in. We do not query email providers or Gravatar, and public avatar addresses do not contain email addresses, email hashes or account IDs.

Comments support Markdown formatting, with the original source stored in the comment database. External images show their source domain first and contact that website only after you choose to load them. The image website receives network information needed for that request, such as your IP address; this website does not send the current page address. Its cookies depend on your browser settings. Load only sources you trust. Previews follow the same rule. Raw HTML is displayed as text; scripts and embedded pages do not execute.

Sign-in sessions normally use browser local storage. Sign out after using a shared device. If storage is unavailable, the session lasts only until the page closes or refreshes. Unsubmitted drafts remain in page memory and may be lost when leaving or refreshing. Signing out or changing accounts clears the previous account’s draft; a session refresh for the same account or a network retry preserves it.

Deleting your comment clears its content and leaves a deletion notice, nickname, timestamp and reply relationships; other people’s replies remain. The owner may remove abusive discussions and their replies or restrict posting. Private duplicate-prevention fingerprints and operational metadata may remain; provider logs or backups follow their maintenance cycles. Clearing a comment does not immediately erase every historical copy. For account deletion or other personal data requests, contact the owner below; identity verification may be needed.

Your light or dark theme preference is stored locally in your browser for future visits. Site search queries a static index in your browser; this website has no backend that receives your search terms.

Public GitHub contribution data is fetched when the website is built and then displayed as static content. Article image zoom scripts are served with this website. Hosting and external resource providers receive the information needed to complete network requests. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for GitHub's practices. External links lead to websites with their own practices.

Providers receive network information needed to deliver and protect their services, which may include IP and device information. The comment API does not publish those details. See the [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) and [Resend Privacy Policy](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy).
